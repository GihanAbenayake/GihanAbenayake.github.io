While working at The Earth Village Opportunity Shop, I've had the chance to further develop my computing skills through a variety of experiences

Software Administration Support

As a Technology, Office Support and Customer Service Volunteer, I've been given the opportunity to learn how software administration and IT support is handled in an office environment.

In this case, I've helped to rollout ConnectTeams as the shops new communication and timetabling tool while also decomissioning the old tool, Calendly. Mostly by helping to create channels for communication and assisting in the installation and authentication of the new software.

Metrics Dashboard Optimisation

While working for the Op Shop, I came across a need to improve the efficiency of data entry regarding the shops goods metrics. And in order to do so, I searched through the various corrupted entries to find out their errors and correct them.

In doing so, I found out that the errors were caused by null entries during submission of the form and in order to prevent them in the future, I suggested and implemented null entry checks. As such, no form entries have needed to be corrected for missing values during submission which has improved the efficiency of Office Support tasks massively, while also improving the reliability of the metrics.

Digital Forms and Automations

I've also been given the opportunity to improve the automation and usage of digital forms while working at The Earth Village Op Shop.

In this case, I've had to learn how to use Clickup's automations in order improve the tracking process for the impact statistics of the shops deliveries. While doing so, I was given the opportunity to improve the forms currently being used, adding in automations to check for null fields and fill them with the correct data, so as to not break the dashboards used to represent the shops data in a visual format.

This has in turn helped to improve the shops data quality and operational efficiency by meaning that not all fields of certain forms have to be filled in on submission and allowing more data to be digitized for representation in a more user friendly manner.

Office Support and Customer Service

My time at the Op Shop has also led to me adopting Office Support roles and Customer service roles to assist with the administration of the shop.

In doing so, I've assisted with the recording of deliveries and other logistical data, and I've also assisted with the creation of dashboards to represent the data in a visual format. As well as having helped to digitize 18 months worth of physical data into the system and having created signage for the shop.

This has also helped to improve the reliability of the data and overall efficiency of the shop while helping to guide customers to the items they would like most, improving the customer experience.
